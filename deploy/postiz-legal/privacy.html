Privacy Policy

Nourbit · Nour Daily · postiz.nourdaily.com

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nour Daily (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our self-hosted social media scheduling service at https://postiz.nourdaily.com (the “Service”), including the Nourbit publishing workflow operated through this Service.

1. Who operates the Service

The Service is operated by Nour Daily for internal content operations and authorized team members. The Service is not offered as a public multi-tenant SaaS product.

2. Information we collect

Depending on which platforms you connect, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email, and login credentials for the Service itself.
  • TikTok (Login Kit & Content Posting API) — OAuth access and refresh tokens, open ID, display name, avatar, profile information, and account statistics when you connect your TikTok creator account. We also process video files, captions, and publishing metadata you submit for scheduling or direct posting.
  • Other social platforms — similar OAuth tokens and profile identifiers for channels you choose to connect (for example YouTube, Facebook, Telegram).
  • Technical data — server logs (IP address, timestamps, request metadata) required to operate and secure the Service.

3. How we use information

We use collected information only to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your session on the Service.
  • Connect and manage social media integrations you authorize.
  • Schedule, publish, or upload content you explicitly approve.
  • Display connected account identity in the dashboard (for example channel name and avatar).
  • Monitor delivery status, errors, and operational health of the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against abuse.
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use TikTok data for advertising profiles or unrelated analytics products.

4. Legal basis & your control

We process data based on your consent when you connect a social account and confirm a publish action. You control what is posted: content is only sent to TikTok or other platforms after you schedule or confirm publishing in the Service.

You may disconnect any integration at any time from the Service settings. Disconnecting revokes our ability to post on your behalf using stored tokens.

5. Data storage & retention

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by Nour Daily (self-hosted servers). OAuth tokens and scheduling records are retained while your integration remains active and as needed to complete pending publishes. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us (see Section 9).

6. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with:

  • Platform APIs you connect (for example TikTok, YouTube, Meta, Telegram) to perform actions you request.
  • Infrastructure providers (for example hosting, DNS, tunneling) that process data solely to deliver the Service.

We do not share data with data brokers or unrelated third-party marketers.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS, access controls, and restricted server access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect data proportionate to the sensitivity of OAuth credentials and media assets.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Contact & data requests

For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact: sahibulnf@nourdaily.com

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the Service after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
