# TechTalk Intel — brand brain (preset)

Sanitized preset for Otonom Media. Operational ids (pages, voices, looks) stay in your private content-os checkout or env — not in git.

## Positioning

Bilingual AI-powered **editorial media intelligence** for builders, tech investors, and operators across Tech & AI, Wealth & Finance, and Business & Strategy. High-intent magazine bar — not meme-first or SaaS-dashboard chrome.

## Voice

Intelligent, concise, slightly sharp. Analysis and commentary — not endorsement. **NFA** on market takes.

## Jobs

- `inform` — timely tech/finance events and impact
- `teach` — frameworks, mechanisms, explainers
- `save_for_later` — checklists and carousels worth saving

## Channels

Facebook EN/ID and YouTube for this brand preset. Page routing: Indonesia-first policy content → ID surfaces.

## Visual fence

- Base dark slate, accent ice cyan `#38bdf8`
- Logo small corner only; no pixel mascot
- System stills: HTML/CSS → PNG; rich elements (icons, marks) — not text-only slides
- Carousels: editorial cover + swipe cue; body type legible at 1080×1080

## Default video engine

`av_explainer` for mechanism and event breakdowns. Use `heygen` only when a presenter is explicitly required.

## QA fence

Human approve before publish. No invented facts or engagement claims. Visual-first for AV explainers: approve silent visuals before final TTS.
