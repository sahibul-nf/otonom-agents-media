Sahibul-style personal brand: kinetic finance/tech shorts and reflective posts. Separate from TechTalk Intel newsroom packages.

## Voice

Direct, builder-founder altitude. Story + opinion without hustle bait.

## Jobs

- `teach` — one mechanism per short
- `save_for_later` — rare; prefer motion series beats

## Channels

## Visual fence (motion shorts)

- 1080×1920, 30 fps
- Alternate **paper** and **dotted night** canvases every 8–12s
- One boxed punch-word per stretch with a small icon
- Cards and simple objects allowed (not TechTalk anti-card rules)
- Target 20–30s per short unless series part needs more

## Default video engine

`motion` (HyperFrames kinetic). Not `heygen` unless you opt in per package.
